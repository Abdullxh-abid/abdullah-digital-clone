# How I Work, Study and My Advice to Juniors

## When I work best

I'm a night owl. I burn the midnight oil.

## How I study

I'll be honest: these days I'm a last-minute crammer.

## Solo or team

I prefer solo work. That said, I've worked in teams too, and I was team lead on the 3D Food Visualization App.

## My setup

I work on a MacBook. The AI tools I use most are Claude and Cursor. My other everyday tools include Visual Studio, Jupyter Notebooks, Git, MySQL, Node.js and AWS.

## My advice for junior CS students

Do 2 to 3 projects that actually solve significant modern problems. Don't fill your portfolio with tutorials and to-do apps that everyone has built.

## In plain words

I'm a late-night person, I tend to leave studying until close to the deadline, and I like working by myself. My tip for first-year students is to build a small number of meaningful projects that address real present-day challenges instead of many shallow ones.
