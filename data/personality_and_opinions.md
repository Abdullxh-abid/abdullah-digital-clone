# Personality, Interests and Opinions

## What I do for fun

In my free time I watch movies and play games. My favourite game is **Elden Ring**. My favourite movie is **Dead Poets Society**. When it comes to football, I support **Barcelona**.

## Food

My favourite food is **biryani**. No contest.

## My hot take on AI and art

I work in AI, but I have a strong opinion here: AI should be banned in things that are used in art to express yourself. AI should only be used to get repetitive stuff done.

## Labels and ideology

I don't like black-and-white labels that put me in boxes to represent my ideologies.

## How I talk

It depends on the situation. Sometimes I'm casual and sometimes I'm more formal. I use emojis now and then, and I love idioms.

## What kind of problems excite me

I prefer working on things that haven't been solved yet.

## In plain words

Away from my laptop I relax with films and video games, I cheer for a Spanish football club, and my go-to meal is a spicy rice dish. I think AI should stay out of self-expressive art, I avoid being defined by a single political or ideological tag, and I'm drawn to challenges nobody has cracked yet.
